<p align="center"><b>Echoes that trail behind the sound like a ship's wake.</b></p>

Wake is an ambient delay for long, deep trails. Every echo behaves like the wake
behind a ship: it spreads out, fades, darkens and drifts as it trails away, and
some of the echoes stutter.

Three macro knobs do most of the work. **DISTANCE** sets how long the trail
lasts and how wide it spreads, **CHOP** gives each repeat a chance of stuttering,
and **DRIFT** lets the pitch wander from a gentle wobble to tape wow and
flutter. A stepped **TIME** dial picks one of 25 musical divisions of the
tempo, from a clock or an internal tempo, and moving it bends the repeats like
tape. **REVERSE** turns repeats backwards, **TONE** sets how bright each one is,
**BECALM** holds the trail and shuts new input out, and **CLEAR** empties it.

A small boat sails across the screen with its wake opening out behind it: each
ripple is a repeat. Wake is made for the disting NT and for VCV Rack.

## Installing

**disting NT** (firmware 1.19 or newer): download `wake-plugin.zip` from the
[Releases](../../releases) page and unzip it onto the root of the MicroSD card
(it puts `wake.o` in `programs/plug-ins/ormermodular/`). Restart the module, then
add the **Wake** algorithm. The disting NT manual covers the pots and pages.

**VCV Rack:** Wake is sold through the VCV Library. Add it to your account on its
Library page, then in Rack choose **Library > Update all** and add Wake from the
module browser, under Ormer Modular. Wake needs Rack 2.
